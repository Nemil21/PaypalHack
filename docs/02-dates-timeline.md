## Discrepancies to be aware of
- **Winner announcement time:** Dates page says Dec 21 **9:00 am PST**; Official Rules say "on or around Monday, Dec 21, 2026 (**2:00 pm** Pacific Time)". Rules prevail if there's a conflict (Rules §11).
- **Prize total:** banner says "$67,500 in cash"; body text and updates say "$69,750". Difference = $2,250 in Render credits (non-cash).

## Post-deadline obligations
- Submissions lock at the deadline (you can still edit the project in your Devpost *portfolio*, but not the submission).
- Organizers: **don't edit your repo, video, or linked materials until after the Dec 21 announcement.**
- Keep the demo, test credentials, and any trials/credits **active until Dec 21** (rules require free access through the end of the Judging Period, Dec 15; organizers ask through Dec 21).
- Watch out: many sponsor trials are 14–45 days (Elastic 14d + 30d extension, Zapier 14d, AG Grid 45d, Bryntum 45d). Make sure the hosted demo doesn't depend on something that expires before Dec 21.
- If you win: prize affidavit/Required Forms are due **10 business days** after they're sent; prizes delivered within 60 days of receipt of forms. US: W-9; non-US: W-8BEN.
