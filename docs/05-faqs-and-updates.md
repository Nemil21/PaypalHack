## FAQs (all, verbatim-ish)

**Is there a maximum number of members per team?**
No. Enter solo, as a team, or as an organization. Teams pick one Representative who submits. You can be on more than one team and still enter on your own.

**Can I create a PayPal developer account from my country?**
You'll use the free PayPal sandbox with an existing PayPal account or a free account at developer.paypal.com. Sign-up options vary by country; if you can't sign up (country/phone not offered), ask the PayPal team on Discord. Being able to open a PayPal account is separate from hackathon eligibility (Rules §3).

**I live in Brazil. Am I eligible?**
No. Rules §3 excludes places where US or local law prohibits participation/prizes — Brazil specifically, plus Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and any OFAC comprehensively sanctioned country. **Applies to every team member.** Registering on Devpost does not make you eligible.

**How do I get access to the sponsor tools, and can I use more than one?**
Each sponsor's page explains access (see doc 08). Sponsor tools are optional; any AI tool works if PayPal is central. You can use several and be considered for each sponsor prize, but a project can win **at most one Sponsor Prize plus either one Grand Prize or one Honorable Mention** (§8). Help: Discord.

**How do I join a team or add teammates?**
Through the Devpost project, not an email accept button: everyone registers → one person starts the project → invite by email or share the team invite link → each teammate opens it logged in and accepts.
