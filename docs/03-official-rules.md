## §3 Eligibility
**Open to:** individuals at/above age of majority where they live; Teams of such individuals; Organizations (companies, nonprofits, LLCs, partnerships…) existing at time of entry.
- An individual may be on multiple teams and also enter solo.
- Teams/Orgs must appoint one **Representative** who submits on their behalf.

**NOT open to:**
- Residents of places where US or local law prohibits participating/receiving a prize — **including Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea**, and any OFAC comprehensively sanctioned country. Applies to **every team member**. Registering on Devpost ≠ eligible.
- Organizations involved in running the hackathon (PayPal, Devpost = "Promotion Entities"), their employees/representatives/agents and their immediate family/household.
- Judges, or companies/individuals employing a judge (note: sponsor companies like AG Grid, APIMatic, Bryntum, Channel3, Elastic, Postman, Render employ judges).
- Parent/subsidiary/affiliates of the above; anyone creating a real or apparent conflict of interest.

## §4 How to enter
1. Register ("Join Hackathon") with a free Devpost account.
2. Get access to the dev tools (subject to their licenses). Sandbox: existing PayPal account or free at https://developer.paypal.com/.
3. Complete all required fields on "Enter a Submission" during the Submission Period.

### Project requirements
- **What to create:** build or update an app integrating the **PayPal developer platform (free sandbox)** + an **AI tool/model/platform** of your choice. All PayPal services count (Venmo, Braintree, Xoom, Honey, Zettle, Paidy, Hyperwallet, Simility, Swift Financial…).
- Sponsor tools optional; "any AI tool works, as long as PayPal integration is central."
- Sponsor-prize entries must also meet §8 requirements (i.e., actually use the sponsor's tool).
- **Functionality:** must install and run consistently on the intended platform, and function as depicted in the video/description.
- **Platforms:** must run on the platform specified in the submission.
- **New & existing:** new, or *significantly updated* after Oct 1 — explain how. Sponsor/Devpost decide what counts as "significant."
- **Third-party integrations:** must be authorized under their terms/licenses.

### Submission requirements
- Project meeting the above.
- Text description of features and functionality.
- **Functional demo** — doesn't need to be publicly hosted, but judges must be able to run/interact with a working build: complete setup/run instructions in the repo, **or** a hosted demo URL. Mockups/static/non-functional demos don't qualify.
- **Public code repo** URL (GitHub, Bitbucket, GitLab) — all source, assets, instructions; **public + open source license file detectable at the top of the repo page (About section).**
- **Demo video:** < 3 minutes (judges not required to watch beyond); footage of the project functioning on its device; **public on YouTube**, link on the form; no third-party trademarks or copyrighted music/material without permission.

### Other entry rules
- **Multiple submissions** allowed if each is unique and substantially different.
- **Ownership:** original work, solely owned, no IP violations.
- **Testing access:** provide a website, functioning demo, or test build. Private site → include **login credentials in testing instructions**. Must be **free and unrestricted** for Sponsor/Admin/Judges until Judging ends. Judges **may judge solely from description, images, and video.**
  - Include setup/run instructions **plus any test account credentials, API keys, or sandbox login details** needed. Special access → clear instructions in README or description.
  - Non-standard hardware (not phone/tablet/desktop) → may be asked for physical access.
- **Language:** English, or provide English translations of video, description, testing instructions, everything.
- **Team Representation:** one eligible Representative submits.
- **IP:** your/team's original work, solely owned, not infringing. You may hire third-party technical help if components are your work product and ideas. Open source allowed if you comply with licenses and **build upon/enhance** it.
- **Financial/preferential support:** project must not have been developed with funding, contract, or commercial license from PayPal or Devpost before the end of the Submission Period.

## §5 Submission modifications
- Drafts can be saved before submitting. After the period ends, **no changes** to the Submission (portfolio can still be updated).
- Post-deadline edits only if Sponsor/Devpost permit (e.g., removing infringing material or PII); must remain substantively the same.

## §6 Judges & criteria
- Sponsor/Admin decide eligibility & methodology; may use expert panels, peer review, **automated AI-driven analysis**, or combinations. Judges may be PayPal employees or third parties; may be unlisted; may change; multiple rounds possible.
- **Stage One (pass/fail):** baseline viability — reasonably fits the theme and reasonably applies the required APIs/SDKs.
- **Stage Two:** equally weighted:
  - **Technological Implementation** — How thoroughly and skillfully does the project use PayPal Developer Platform and AI tool(s)? Genuine effort and a working, non-trivial implementation?
  - **Design** — A complete, coherent product experience, not just a technical PoC?
  - **Potential Impact** — Credible, specific case for solving a real problem for a real audience — and does the demonstrated solution actually address it?
  - **Innovation/Idea** — How creative and novel; differs from existing concepts?
  - **Presentation** — Does the video clearly show it working end-to-end? Does the pitch say what problem, for whom, and why it matters? Easy to follow?
- **Tie-breaking:** highest score on first criterion (Tech Implementation), then next criterion, etc.; then a judges' vote.

- You warrant content isn't infringing and contains no malware.

## Rules-driven gotchas for our team
- **Every** team member must be eligible (check residency — e.g., Brazil/Quebec are out).
- **License in the repo's About section** — add a LICENSE file (MIT/Apache-2.0) so GitHub detects it.
- **Credentials:** judges need sandbox test logins/keys to run it, but never commit secrets — put test-only sandbox creds in the Devpost testing instructions or a clearly marked section, and use env vars.
- **Video music:** use royalty-free or no music; avoid showing third-party brand logos you don't have rights to.
- If building on an existing project, **explicitly state what was built during Oct 1–Nov 12**.
