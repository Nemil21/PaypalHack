# Pay Later overview (/pay-later/overview)



PayPal offers financing options that let your customers buy now and pay later, while you get paid up front. Specific Pay Later offers vary by country. For more information about Pay Later, see [Buy now, pay later](https://www.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/ways-to-pay/buy-now-pay-later).

To add Pay Later products to an integration with a third-party commerce platform, see [Commerce Platforms](/v5/pay-later/commerce-platforms). For information about how to use PayPal's JavaScript SDK for a low-code Pay Later integration, see [Get started with Pay Later](/pay-later/integrate).

With Pay Later, you can get:

* **Increased conversion:** Adding "buy now, pay later" messaging to your site can improve conversion, attract new customers, and increase order values.
* **Dynamic messages:** You can show your customers a Pay Later product that is based on the contents of their shopping cart.
* **Multiple touch points:** Add messaging throughout your site such as on product pages, the shopping cart, and checkout pages.

Pay Later products vary by locale. To view details, select a country.

**United States**

In the US, Pay Later includes 2 products:

| Product     | Number of payments | Frequency                | Purchase amount |
| ----------- | ------------------ | ------------------------ | --------------- |
| Pay in 4    | 4                  | Every 2 weeks (biweekly) | $10 to $2,000   |
| Pay Monthly | 3, 6, 12, or 24    | Monthly                  | $49 to $10,000  |

**Eligibility**

You're eligible to integrate Pay Later products in the US if you meet all of the following requirements:

* Are a US-based PayPal merchant.
* Have a US-facing website.
* Transact in US dollars (USD).
* Have a [PayPal one-time payment](/checkout) integration.
* Abide by the [Pay Later Messaging Center Program Terms](https://www.paypal.com/legalhub/paypal/pay-later-messaging-tnc?locale.x=en_US&_ga=2.230264284.1105075193.1757947850-1032254505.1740765755).

Render the Pay Later messaging in its entirety with all the links and language provided by PayPal. For noncompliant messaging, PayPal reserves the right to take action in accordance with the [PayPal User Agreement](https://www.paypal.com/legalhub/paypal/useragreement-full).
* Do not create, display, or host your own Pay Later content. Instead, integrate the official code that PayPal provides.

**United Kingdom**

In the United Kingdom, Pay Later includes 2 products:

* With **Pay in 30 Days**, eligible UK buyers can pay the full amount in 30 days with no interest or fees. Pay in 30 Days is offered for purchases of £1 to £900 and requires no down payment. Buyers may have the option to get more time to pay should they need additional time to repay.

| -------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
